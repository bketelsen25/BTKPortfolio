# Reflections

My decision-making process was most prominent during Major Assignment 2 because I had far more freedom to choose my study topic and therefore had to do more consideration. For Major Assignment 1, the network map concept was a main focus of the project, and my work mostly consisted of fulfilling that specific goal. This time, the topic of my study, the methods with which I would conduct it, and the conclusions to be drawn from the results would all need to be created by me. I chose to use AI as my study subject both because of my personal interest in proper AI usage and prompt engineering, and also admittedly the relative simplicity of it compared to involving other people, potentially requiring a training for studying human participants, etc.
